What I track

Short version: I use Piwik PRO to see how many people visit joleksiak.pl and what they look at. I don't sell your data or use it for advertising. Piwik PRO processes analytics data on my behalf. This page does not load analytics scripts.

Who is responsible

Jan Oleksiak, the owner of this site. Contact: click to email me.

If you agree to analytics

  • page views, scroll depth, link clicks and time spent on the page,
  • heatmaps of how the page is used,
  • technical data: browser, device type, screen size, referring site,
  • your IP address with the last part removed (e.g. 203.0.113.0 instead of 203.0.113.42) and approximate location (country/city),
  • cookies: _pk_id.* (recognises returning visitors, up to about 13 months) and _pk_ses.* (current visit, 30 minutes).

Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR).

If you decline

Nothing is stored on your device for analytics: no analytics cookies, no local storage. Piwik PRO still records your visit in its anonymous tracking mode: page views, scroll depth (25/50/75/100%), link clicks, time on page, technical data and your interaction with the consent banner. IP is fully anonymized. Returning visitors are not recognized.

Legal basis: my legitimate interest in basic visit statistics (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Either way

  • Your choice is stored in the ppms_privacy_* cookie for 1 year, so the banner doesn't ask you on every visit.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Fonts are served from this site, not from Google.

Where the data goes and for how long

Analytics data is processed by Piwik PRO and hosted by Elastx in Sweden (EU). GitHub is based in the USA. Transfers to GitHub rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses.

Analytics data in Piwik PRO is kept for 25 full months. After that it is deleted automatically.

Your rights

  • You can withdraw or change your consent at any time with the small cookie icon in the bottom-left corner of the main page. Withdrawing consent doesn't affect processing that happened before.
  • You can ask me for access to your data, correction, deletion, restriction of processing or data portability, and you can object to processing based on legitimate interest. Just email me.
  • You can also complain directly to a data protection authority. In Poland that's the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO).
